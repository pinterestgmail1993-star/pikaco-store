FAQ

Quick answers

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp with the design you want. We confirm the details, send a preview, and start printing after your approval.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery anywhere in Pakistan. If you prefer, pay in advance via JazzCash or EasyPaisa — we verify every transfer in our own app before dispatch.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 2 to 3 days after design approval, via courier. You receive a tracking number as soon as it ships.

Can I request custom designs beyond the four products?

Yes. Send us your idea — logos, dates, quotes, shapes. Custom work starts from Rs. 699 depending on size.
