Good questions

Everything you asked us

How much does a customized keychain cost in Pakistan?

Most of our keychains are between Rs 449 and Rs 799, and the name or number customization is always free. Figurines and phone stands go up to Rs 1399. No hidden charges, what you see is what you pay.

How does Cash on Delivery work?

You order on WhatsApp, we print your item fresh, and it reaches your door in two to three working days anywhere in Pakistan. You pay the courier when it is in your hands. Simple as that.

Can you print MY name or my bike number?

Yes yaar, that is literally our favourite thing. Send us the name, number or design idea on WhatsApp and we will show you a preview before printing. It usually takes one extra day.

How long does delivery take?

Two to three working days for Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and basically every city in between. Customized orders take about one day longer because we print them fresh just for you.

What are the products made of?

PLA+ filament, a sturdy plant-based material printed in super fine layers. Colors are baked in so nothing fades, and the prints are chunky enough to survive handbags, bike rides and little cousins.

Ready to meet your new favourite thing?

One WhatsApp message. That is the whole process. We are fast, we are nice, and we reply in proper Roman Urdu if you prefer.
