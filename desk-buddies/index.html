Good questions

Everything you asked us

Do these figurines tip over?

Nope. Every desk buddy has a weighted base because we learned that lesson the hard way with early prototypes. They stay planted even if you bump the desk.

Can I get a custom color?

Usually yes. WhatsApp us the buddy and the color you want, if the filament exists, we will print it. Special colors sometimes take one extra day.

What size are they?

Between 8 and 10 centimeters tall, chunky and noticeable on a desk but small enough to not eat your workspace. Exact sizes are on each product page.

Can’t decide? Neither can we.

Send us a WhatsApp message and we will help you pick, send extra photos, or design something from scratch.
