Are these stickers waterproof?

Yes, every sheet is waterproof vinyl that handles rain, chai spills and water bottles. Colors do not fade in the sun, and the die-cut edges never peel from corners like cheap stickers do.

What is the sticker sheet price in Pakistan?

Between Rs 399 and Rs 549 depending on the pack, with around ten designs per sheet. COD delivery nationwide takes two to three days, one sheet or ten.

Can you make a custom sticker design?

Usually yes. WhatsApp us your idea, logo or inside joke and we will honestly tell you if we can print it well. Custom packs take about one extra day.
